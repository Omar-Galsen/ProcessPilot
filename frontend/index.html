Interactive lesson: Bernoulli's equation

Explore an ideal, steady, incompressible flow between two points. Adjust the pipe diameters, inlet pressure, elevation, and flow rate.

P₁ + ½ρv₁² + ρgz₁ = P₂ + ½ρv₂² + ρgz₂

Idealized educational model: horizontal inlet reference, no pump/turbine, friction, or minor losses. Negative outlet gauge pressure is possible in the calculation; real systems may cavitate or violate the model assumptions.
